Product

Build the way that fits the page.

Elementiq is a powerful WordPress website builder that combines visual drag and drop design with AI assisted coding. Design visually when you want speed and simplicity. Use Studio when you want AI assisted coding and deeper control. Both stay inside WordPress.

A flexible WordPress building environment

Some pages are faster by hand. Some pages are faster when you describe them. Elementiq keeps both options on the same install, on the same page, without moving the site somewhere else.

Design visually when you want speed and simplicity

Open Design. Drag widgets, arrange layouts, and style content on the canvas. You see every change as you make it. Coding knowledge is not required.

Use Studio when you want AI assisted coding and deeper control

Open Studio. Describe the page. AI can write HTML and CSS. Then click the live preview to refine the result before you save.

What we will not claim

Not a host

Elementiq is a plugin. Your pages, media, and permalinks stay on your WordPress website.

Not one editor with two names

Design and Studio are different workflows. Use the one that matches the job.

Not unlimited free AI

Studio uses the provider and key you connect. Pricing and limits belong to that provider.
