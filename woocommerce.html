The partnership

Beauty in Elementiq. Commerce in Woo.

A pretty shop that cannot checkout is a brochure. A checkout that cannot be designed is a form. Together they are a store: the window display and the register, in the same dashboard.

EQ

What Elementiq brings

The Design canvas, Studio chat, live preview, brand, header, and the courage to say “build me a store” in plain language. Elementiq makes the shop feel like your brand.

WC

What WooCommerce brings

Products, prices, stock, variations, cart, checkout, taxes, shipping, payments, and orders. WooCommerce is the store engine. Elementiq does not replace it.

When you prompt “create an online store,” Studio and Design are instructed to use WooCommerce widgets and shortcodes. Static cards with dead Add to cart buttons are not a store.

From prompt to first sale

01

You ask for a store

In Studio or Design, describe the shop: candles, clothes, records. The AI treats that as a WooCommerce job, not a landing-page collage.

02

Elementiq lays the floor

Hero, grid, cart in the header. Design drops wooproducts. Studio writes [products], [woocommerce_cart], and checkout shortcodes.

03

WooCommerce runs the till

Add products in WooCommerce. Visitors add to cart and pay through Woo. Orders land in WordPress. The page still looks like Elementiq.

How to start

Install both. Then ask for a store.

Activate WooCommerce and Elementiq on the same WordPress site. Add a few products. Open Pages, choose Design or Studio, and say what you sell. The AI is taught to reach for WooCommerce instead of inventing a fake till.
