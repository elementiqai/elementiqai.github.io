Connect an AI provider for Studio.

AI features in Elementiq need a configured provider and API key. Open Elementiq → Settings, add your key, and test it. Design still works without a key. What Studio can generate depends on the provider you connect. Providers are not automatically free.

How to configure AI

01

Open Settings

In the WordPress dashboard, go to Elementiq → Settings.

02

Choose a provider

Pick Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Kimi, or a custom endpoint that your team already uses.

03

Paste the API key

Copy the key from the provider’s dashboard. Store it in Elementiq Settings, then test the connection.

04

Open Studio

Create or open a page, switch to Studio, and send a request. If generation fails, check the key and provider status.

What to expect

Your keys stay on your site

You bring the key. Elementiq does not replace your WordPress site with a hosted AI product.

Results vary by provider

Models, rate limits, and pricing belong to the provider. Elementiq uses the key you configured.

Pages still save in WordPress

If a provider is busy or a request fails, the page you already built remains on your site.

Layout templates such as full width, canvas, or theme boxed are also in Settings. They control how new pages sit in your theme, not which AI model you use.
